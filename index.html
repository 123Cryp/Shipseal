You become the client (party A) and your stake is escrowed now. The developer (party B) must match it exactly to accept. Everything below is recorded on-chain before anyone knows how a dispute will go.

Plain text. Once every structured check passes, a model checks the release metadata against this. It can only confirm or withhold; it cannot award the pot on its own.

Use the canonical form PyPI reports, for example 1.2.0 or 2.0.0rc1.

If at least one required kind is published on time but another is missing, the outcome is partially delivered.

The release metadata (project URLs or home page) must link to this repository.

Deadline between 1 hour and 90 days from now. Evidence host is fixed to pypi.org.
